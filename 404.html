The rest of the service list

The landing page covers the two doors. Individual RCM stations and remaining specialties continue below—and in full on the Services pages.

  • Front-cycleEligibility & benefitsReal-time and batch eligibility so visits are scheduled, authorized, and collected against the right plan.
  • Front-cyclePrior authorizationPayer-specific auth intake, clinical packet assembly, and status chase so procedures are not delayed or denied.
  • Mid-cycleMedical codingICD-10-CM, CPT, HCPCS, and E/M coding with specialty guidelines, NCCI, and LCD/NCD awareness.
  • Mid-cycleCharge capture & entryComplete, timely charges from the EHR or superbills—with missing-charge detection before the claim leaves.
  • Back-cycleClaims submissionScrubbed 837P/837I claims through clearinghouses, with edit resolution before the payer ever sees the file.
  • Back-cycleDenial managementRoot-cause denial work: appeals, corrected claims, and front-end fixes so the same CARC does not recur.
  • Back-cycleA/R follow-upPayer A/R worked by aging bucket, dollar threshold, and inventory rules—not by whoever shouts loudest.
  • Back-cyclePayment postingERA/EFT and paper remits posted accurately, with underpayments and takebacks identified the same cycle.
  • Back-cyclePatient billingClear statements, polite outreach, and payment-plan handling after insurance has adjudicated.
  • Enablement-cycleProvider credentialingEnrollment, CAQH, payer contracts, and recredentialing so claims are not held for an inactive provider.
  • Enablement-cycleAnalytics & reportingOperating dashboards for clean-claim rate, denial mix, DSO, net collection, and coder productivity.
  • Enablement-cycleRCM assessment & consultingA structured look at leakage, staffing, EHR setup, and payer mix before you outsource or retool.
