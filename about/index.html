About Us

A private limited company built to run US revenue cycle with AI in the loop.

360VERTEXAI SOLUTIONS PRIVATE LIMITED designs and operates eligibility, coding, claims, denials, A/R, and posting for United States providers.

Applied AI

Models flag eligibility gaps, coding variance, and likely denials—never attested codes on their own.

Human accountability

Certified coders and RCM specialists attest every production decision and payer contact.

Seamless integration

We operate inside your EHR and clearinghouse stack with queues configured by specialty.

Healthcare billing fails in the seams: an eligibility miss, a modifier, a clearinghouse edit, a denial nobody categorized. We built 360VERTEXAI to own those seams as one operating system—not twelve vendors forwarding tickets.

We do not publish fictional client logos or invented collection rates. If we engage, you will see your own inventory, your own denial mix, and a weekly pack your CFO can read.
