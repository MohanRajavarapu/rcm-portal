Every station of the revenue cycle, available as a slice or the full loop.

Medical coding

Revenue that was performed is actually billed.

Claims submission

Higher first-pass yield and fewer preventable rejects.

Denial management

Recovered dollars and a shrinking denial mix.

Back
A/R follow-up

Payment posting

Books that match the bank, and underpayments that get worked.

Back
Patient billing
Clear statements, polite outreach, and payment-plan handling after insurance has adjudicated.

Patient collections without damaging the practice brand.
