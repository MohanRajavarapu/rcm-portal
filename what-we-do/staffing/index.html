Services / Staffing & Workforce

Services · Staffing

Scale the team without mixing this up with RCM outsourcing.

Workforce buyers: named operators, pods, EOR, and searches. If you need us to run the cycle, use RCM & AI Operations.

Engagement models

Staff augmentation through direct hire.

Staff Augmentation

We source, screen, and deploy RCM talent who work as an extension of your team. You direct day-to-day work; we remain the employer of record for payroll and statutory coverage.

Contract-to-Hire

A time-boxed contract with a documented conversion path. Useful when you want production output now and a permanent seat later, without guessing from interviews alone.

3rd-Party Payroll / EOR

Employer-of-record and third-party payroll for workers you source or already know. We run payroll, tax, and statutory coverage so you are not improvising multi-state employment.

Direct Hire

Retained or contingency search for full-time RCM roles on your payroll. We recruit and screen; you employ. No ongoing EOR relationship after start.

Dedicated pods

A cross-functional team, not a pile of contractors.

Lead + operators + QA / AI reviewer. Sized to a queue, not a headcount vanity number.

How a pod starts

  1. 01

    Queue scope

    Volume, specialty, systems, and whether anyone will see claims or EHR data.

  2. 02

    Seat the pod

    Named lead, operators, and a QA / AI reviewer. Not a bench of anonymous hours.

  3. 03

    Overlap hours

    Coverage mapped to US Eastern and Pacific business hours before go-live.

  4. 04

    Access only if needed

    If the pod can touch PHI-adjacent data, access waits on your BAA and the data-residency disclosure.
